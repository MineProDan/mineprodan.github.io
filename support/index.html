Adding a widget

  1. Touch and hold an empty spot on your Home Screen.
  2. Tap Edit (top left), then Add Widget.
  3. Search for WidgetX, pick a widget and size, and tap Add Widget.

For the Lock Screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize → Lock Screen, then tap the widget area.

Common questions

My widget isn’t updating every second.
iOS limits how often widgets refresh, for every app. WidgetX saves new values when you open the app and a few times an hour otherwise. Countdowns (like “next class in 14 min”) are kept current by iOS itself.

Why do CPU and memory only change while the app is open?
WidgetX only reads them while the app is on screen, which saves battery. Apps can’t keep sampling in the background. The System Overview widget shows the last reading with its time.

Why doesn’t WidgetX show battery health or GPU usage?
iOS doesn’t give apps access to those.

My classes don’t show up.
Check that Calendar access is on for WidgetX (iPhone Settings → Apps → WidgetX) and that you picked your school calendars in the Student tab. Recurring weekly events are treated as classes. You can relabel any event, and the fix applies to the whole series.

How do I match my wallpaper?
Aesthetic → Theme Sync → pick your wallpaper photo. iOS doesn’t let apps read the wallpaper directly, so you choose the photo. Applying a theme recolours all your WidgetX widgets.

Is WidgetX free?
Yes, completely. There are no subscriptions or in-app purchases, and every widget and theme is included.

Delete my data.
WidgetX → Settings → Delete All Data. See the Privacy Policy.
