The short version

WidgetX doesn’t collect your personal data. There’s no account, no ads and no tracking. Your data stays on your iPhone, and anything that syncs goes through your own iCloud account, which we can’t see.

What the app uses, and where it stays

WhatWhyWhere it stays
Calendar (only if you allow it)To find your classes for the Student widgetsRead on your iPhone. We save event identifiers and your own labels (like course names), never the events themselves. We never change your calendar.
Photos you pickPhoto widget and Theme SyncResized copies are stored on your iPhone. We never see your photo library; you choose each photo.
Notifications (only if you allow them)Class remindersScheduled on your iPhone.
Battery, storage, heat, CPU and memory readingsTech section and widgetsCalculated and stored on your iPhone. Recent readings are kept for 7 days, then deleted.
Your courses, themes, reminder settings and widget settingsSo they appear on your other devicesSynced with Apple’s iCloud private database, in your iCloud account. We can’t access it.
PurchasesPremiumHandled by Apple. We only see whether Premium is active on your device.

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell, share or rent any data.
  • We don’t use advertising or analytics SDKs, and we don’t track you across apps or websites.
  • We don’t run our own servers holding your data.

Network requests the app makes

  • Apple services: iCloud (CloudKit) sync and the App Store (purchases).
  • Feature settings file: the app downloads a small settings file (flags.json) from this website, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. The request contains no personal information. Like any web request, GitHub can see your IP address; we have no access to GitHub’s request logs. See GitHub’s Privacy Statement.

Crash reports

If you’ve chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS Settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash and performance reports. You control this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements.

Deleting your data

Open WidgetX → Settings → Delete All Data. This removes the app’s data from your iPhone and deletes WidgetX’s data from your iCloud. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data. You can also manage iCloud data in Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage.

Children

WidgetX isn’t directed at children under 13 and doesn’t knowingly collect data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the effective date above. If a change affects what data leaves your device, we’ll mention it in the app’s release notes.
